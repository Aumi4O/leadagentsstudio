Rule Use this Avoid this

09

Objections & crisp answers

Raw infrastructure is not the sellable product. Agencies still need branding, client accounts, onboarding, billing, visibility, and repeatable deployment—the layer buyers compare when choosing a white-label partner.
Visible access improves trust, stickiness, and renewals. When clients can log in, see activity, and understand what they pay for, the offer behaves like software—not a hidden service.
You can—but without a product layer, value stays trapped in hourly delivery. Accounts and reporting make recurring fees easier to justify.
Usually less than one-off builds across scattered vendors: centralized control and templates reduce bespoke firefighting.
Recurring revenue and client-account ownership first; end-client outcomes second. Agency owners filter everything through their P&L and control.
