My story

Taste from Paris. Discipline from running real stores.

My path started in Paris, where I studied fashion design and developed the taste, discipline, and respect for craftsmanship that still shape my work today. From there I built and ran online stores, learned how brands actually grow, and automated the machinery behind that growth — content, SEO, social, email, ads, and visual production.

Today I use AI the same way I use design: not as decoration, but as leverage. I'm based in Haifa and work globally in English, French, and Russian.

What matters to me is simple: make the brand look stronger, make the system move faster, and make the business easier to buy from.
