My story

My path started in Paris, where I studied fashion design and developed the taste, discipline, and respect for craftsmanship that still shape my work today. From there, I built and ran online stores, learned how brands actually grow, and automated the machinery behind that growth — content, SEO, social, email, ads, and visual production. Today I use AI the same way I use design: not as decoration, but as leverage. I’m based in Haifa and work globally in English, French, and Russian.

What matters to me is simple:

  • make the brand look stronger,
  • make the system move faster,
  • and make the business easier to buy from.

The three businesses behind my work

AUMI4

AUMI4 is where fashion, taste, and operations meet. It began with my vision of taking what I learned in Paris and applying AI across the fashion experience. Publicly, AUMI4 presents itself as a luxury platform that pairs fashion with AI, and it also emphasizes trust through authentic sourcing, a partnership with an established Italian distributor, and fulfillment from a Milan-based warehouse. For me, AUMI4 is proof that I don’t just talk about AI systems — I build and run them in a live business.

AI Fashion Artists

AI Fashion Artists is my creative studio and living portfolio. It is where I create editorial-grade visuals, campaign imagery, paid-social suites, lookbooks, short motion, and brand-specific visual systems that are meant to look beautiful and perform commercially. The public positioning is very clear: this is AI creative shaped by a Paris-trained eye, built to look editorial and sell like ads. That combination — taste plus commercial clarity — is at the center of everything I do.

Lead Agents Studio

Lead Agents Studio is where I turned that same operator mindset into AI growth systems. Its current offer combines owner-avatar content, UGC-style creative, custom b-roll, and fast AI follow-up so businesses can move from attention to conversation to booking without letting warm demand die in silence. In other words: it is not “AI for the sake of AI.” It is AI used to close the gap between marketing and conversion.

Why this matters for SmartLine

SmartLine exists because I kept seeing the same expensive problem across businesses:

they work hard to earn attention,
they spend money to generate interest,
and then they lose the sale in the conversation gap.

A lead comes in.
Nobody answers fast enough.
The follow-up is late.
The prospect moves on.

That is exactly what SmartLine is built to solve. On the page, SmartLine is positioned as an AI voice agent for calls, SMS, and WhatsApp that answers, qualifies, follows up, books, and hands the conversation to your team when needed. It is built to stop warm leads from disappearing while your staff is busy, closed, or off the clock.

So if you are wondering why I am the right person to build or guide a system like this, the answer is simple:

because I care about both sides of the sale.

I care about the brand before the lead arrives.
And I care about the conversation after the lead arrives.

That is a rare combination.
