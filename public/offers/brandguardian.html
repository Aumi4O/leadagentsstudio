The mechanism — two parts that matter

Brand DNA learns your look. Brand Guard refuses to break it.

Brand DNA

It studies you, then becomes you.

From 5–10 of your images, BrandGuardian AI builds a fingerprint of your brand: a dense visual embedding, your exact color palette in perceptual color space, your composition, mood, and style. Then it trains custom adapters so generations come out in your identity — not a generic one.

Brand Guard

Every image is graded before you see it.

Each generation is scored against your brand on six axes — palette accuracy, logo, typography, overall DNA match, and banned content. If something drifts, one click runs a surgical fix; if it's far off, it regenerates. Off-brand work never reaches your feed.

Before you ask

Questions, answered.

How many images do you need to learn my brand?

As few as 5–10 strong reference images to build your Brand DNA fingerprint. More helps, but the system is designed to learn a consistent look from a small, curated set — the kind of images you already have.

What does "kept on-brand automatically" actually mean?

Every generation is scored against your brand — palette match in perceptual color space, logo detection, typography, overall style similarity, and a banned-content check. Small drift is auto-corrected with a surgical edit; large drift triggers a regenerate. You review finished, on-brand work, not raw output.

Can it do video, not just images?

Yes — stills and motion. Storyboard mode keeps a subject consistent across multiple scenes so you can produce real campaigns, not disconnected clips.

Who owns the model and the outputs?

On the custom build, the private model is yours — trained on your identity and deployed for your team. You own the engine, not just the images.

How is this different from Midjourney or a stock generator?

Those make generically beautiful images. BrandGuardian AI makes your images — trained on your DNA and policed against your brand — so consistency is the default, not a lucky prompt.
