Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Will callers know it's AI?

Most don't notice. SmartLine runs on OpenAI's Realtime voice — it speaks naturally, handles interruptions, and stays on-brand. You can also have it disclose that it's an assistant if you prefer.

What does it actually cost to run?

Plans start at $49/mo and include a block of minutes; usage beyond that is $0.07–$0.15/min depending on tier. A typical busy month for a small business runs a few dozen dollars in usage. You're never surprised — credits and overage are shown in your dashboard.

Can it book into my calendar and CRM?

Yes. It integrates with your calendar to set, confirm, and reschedule appointments, and can log leads and conversations to your CRM so nothing is lost.

How is this different from a voicemail or chatbot?

Voicemail records that you missed someone. A chatbot answers questions. SmartLine sells — it qualifies, follows up, books, and hands warm conversations to your team. It's a front-line revenue agent, not a help desk.

I run an agency. Can I resell it?

Yes — there's a white-label program. Give every client their own branded voice agent under your agency, with their own accounts and your own pricing. Book a call to see the agency offer.
