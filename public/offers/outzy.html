Outzy — iOS · A Lead Agents Studio product

The game that gets kids outside.

Outzy turns outdoor time into something children actually want to do. They earn coins for time outside, dress up a virtual buddy, and complete family missions — while parents get a gentle, private way to encourage the habit. No accounts, no GPS, no ads. Just a reason to go out and play.

Inside the app

A small, warm world that rewards stepping outside.

Buddy

A friend to care for

Every child picks a buddy animal that wakes up, cheers, and dances as they spend time outdoors.

Coins

Time outside = rewards

Minutes outdoors earn coins; streaks and daily goals earn bonuses. The habit pays off, visibly.

Wardrobe

Something to unlock

Coins buy hats, outfits, and backgrounds — a collection that grows the more they play outside.

Missions

Family-sized quests

Park trips, beach days, after-school play — gentle missions that turn into real outdoor moments.

Progress

A weekly picture

A simple chart shows the week's outdoor time, so parents can celebrate the streak together.

Share

A card to be proud of

One tap makes a share card — first name and weekly total only. Nothing more leaves the phone.

Built for parents to trust

Private by design. On purpose.

Children's software should be safe before it's clever. Outzy keeps everything on the device — there's no account, no location tracking, no photos, no chat, and no advertising.

It's a parent-owned app: you set it up, you hold the data, and the only thing the child sees is a buddy that's happy they went outside.
