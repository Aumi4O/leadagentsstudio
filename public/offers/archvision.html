The two bad options

Slow and accurate, or fast and fictional. Pick one — until now.

Traditional 3D visualization is faithful to your plan but takes weeks and a studio's budget. AI image tools are fast and gorgeous — and quietly invent walls, resize rooms, and hallucinate furniture that could never be built.

For an architect, a beautiful render of the wrong room is worse than no render at all. It misleads the client and breaks on site.

ArchVision AI refuses the trade-off. Truth vs Beauty: the plan is law, and the AI only adds the light.

The principle that makes it trustworthy

The geometry is signed off once. After that, nothing moves.

Truth

Your plan becomes the scene

Your CAD drawing is converted into a precise 3D scene — real rooms, real openings, real dimensions. This is the spatial truth everything else must obey.

Beauty

AI adds light, not lies

A trained model polishes realism — materials, lighting, mood — locked to the scene's depth so it can refine the look but cannot redesign the space.

Proof

Drift is caught and rejected

An automatic structural check compares every result to the signed-off geometry. If a wall, opening, or hero element shifted, the result is rejected. Not flagged — rejected.

Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Will the render actually match my plan?

That's the entire point. Your plan is converted to a measured 3D scene, and every result is automatically checked against it. If a wall, opening, or key element drifted, the result is rejected before it reaches you. The AI refines light and materials — it cannot move geometry.

What do you need from me to start?

Your CAD/DWG plan and a few reference images of the look you want. We convert the plan to the scene and train on your aesthetic, then deliver.

Can it match my studio's signature style?

Yes. We train a per-project model on your reference work so the output carries your studio's look consistently across every view — not a generic "AI interior" style.

Do you do walkthrough video, or just stills?

Both. Project packages include a rendered walkthrough video. Interactive, real-time walkthroughs you can navigate in a browser are rolling out in the in-house platform.

Can we run it ourselves instead of commissioning projects?

Yes — the in-house platform is in private early access, and we also build the pipeline directly into studios that want to own it. Book a call to scope it.
