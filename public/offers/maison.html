Before you ask

Questions, answered.

What exactly do I own when I buy?

The whole business: the Shopify store and theme, the domain, the brand identity and assets, the product collection and imagery, the supplier connections, and the AI operating system that runs it. You get a live, trading brand — not a template to assemble.

What does "AI-run operations" actually do?

A 24/7 AI assistant handles customer questions and follow-up, AI try-on reduces returns, AI-driven SEO and automated social keep the brand discoverable, and on-brand imagery is generated on demand. It means the store keeps working without a full team behind it.

Do I need ecommerce experience to run it?

No. The point of turnkey is that the hard build and the automations are done. We hand over with a walkthrough so you understand how it runs, and the AI layer carries the day-to-day load.

Can I commission a brand in a different niche?

Yes. Ciel Cashmere is luxury cashmere, but we build to your category and customer — fashion, home, beauty, accessories, and beyond. Book a call to scope it.

How is this priced?

Ready-made brands are priced per listing — Ciel Cashmere is $9,999 turnkey (auction opens at $3,500). Custom builds are scoped to the brand you want; book a call for a quote.
